Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. ImageKit runs entirely in your browser using the HTML canvas. Your files never leave your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

Is there a file or size limit?

There are no artificial limits set by ImageKit. The only constraint is your device's available memory, since conversion happens locally.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes. Add as many images as you like. They convert sequentially to keep your device responsive, then you can download all of them with one click.

Why did a file fail to convert?

Some formats depend on your browser's codec support. If a format is not supported in your browser, the file is marked with an error rather than silently failing.
